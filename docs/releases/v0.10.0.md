# v0.10.0 · Phase 9: full evaluation

Every evaluation compared with a committed baseline, and fraud measured end to end.

## Included

- **Fraud end to end**: the agents evaluation reports where planted fraud ends (12 of 18 claims
  sent to investigation, 6 to review, none settled automatically), decoys sent to investigation
  (0 of 6), and how often the core's fraud score equals the expected one (96%). The gate fails on
  any planted fraud settled automatically.
- **Planted instructions end to end**: decision (90%) and routing on the ten claims with hidden
  instructions, and no unsafe settlement among them.
- **Baseline and scorecard** (ADR 0017): `evals/baseline.json` holds the headline numbers of
  document reading, policy search and the agents, per provider, with the direction that is
  better and a tolerance; `make scorecard` compares the reports with it and fails on a
  regression or a missing report. Tolerances are zero for every count of harm. Runs of another
  provider are shown, never failed; updating the baseline is explicit and reviewed in the diff.
- **CI** writes a scorecard after each evaluation.
- **Tests**: 229 Python, 53 Java.

## Known limitations

- The baseline is keyless: it measures the deterministic parts and the rule-based baselines, not
  a model. A real-model run needs its own baseline.
