# v0.2.0 · Phase 1: synthetic data

A reproducible, fully synthetic dataset with ground truth for every later evaluation.

## Included

- **Product catalog** (`data/policies/catalog.yaml`): seven home and motor products of the fictional
  *Aseguradora Brisa*, with limits, deductibles, waiting periods, exclusions, required documents,
  notice period and fraud-scoring weights in one place.
- **Policy conditions in Spanish** rendered from the catalog, with stable clause ids (`HP-2.1`) and a
  structured clause index for retrieval. Tests fail if the text drifts from the catalog.
- **Generator** (`make data`, seeded): 200 policyholders, 232 policies, 150 claims and about 230
  documents (invoices, estimates, police reports, accident reports), a quarter of them as scanned
  images. Same seed, same bytes.
- **Ground truth per claim**, derived from the generated facts rather than copied from scenario
  labels: decision, payable amount, routing, reasons, clause citations, missing documents, fraud
  signals and score.
- **Hard cases**: 18 planted fraud claims in four patterns (duplicate invoice, documents dated
  before the incident with mismatched items, early claim with round amounts, frequent claims),
  6 decoys with a single weak signal, 30 out-of-cover claims, late notice, missing reports,
  amount adjustments and 10 hidden prompt-injection attempts.
- **ADR 0007** on how the dataset and its labels are built.
